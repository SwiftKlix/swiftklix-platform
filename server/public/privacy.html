SwiftKlix Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

SwiftKlix ("we", "our", or "us") operates the platform at https://swiftklix-platform.onrender.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our volunteer matching and non-profit branch scaling services:

  • Google User Data (OAuth 2.0): When you choose to log in using Google OAuth, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture URL from Google Identity Services. We do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Account & Profile Information: Information you provide when registering directly, such as your name, email address, bio, skills, and city/campus location.
  • Organization & Application Data: Information submitted when applying to start a branch, joining a chapter committee, or registering a non-profit organization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Authenticating your account and maintaining your active session.
  • Allowing you to apply for founding chapter director roles and volunteer positions.
  • Enabling non-profit organization admins to review your branch applications.
  • Displaying your personalized match compatibility scores across registered causes.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

SwiftKlix's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data to train generalized AI/ML models, and we never sell Google user data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party advertisers. Your information is only shared with verified organization administrators when you explicitly submit an application to join or lead their chapter.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your profile and application data for as long as your account is active. You may request immediate and permanent deletion of all your personal data at any time by emailing us at swiftklix1@gmail.com. All deletion requests are processed within 24 hours.
